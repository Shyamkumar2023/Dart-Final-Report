Feeder Subsystem

The Feeder is the "magazine" of the D.A.R.T. system—tasked with housing three 260g projectiles and executing a precise, autonomous loading sequence in under 2 seconds.

Spatial Constraint Analysis

Intentional Sequential Design

The feeder was the final subsystem to be developed. This was a strategic choice: by confirming the Yaw, Pitch, and Launcher geometries first, we identified the exact "Volume Envelope" remaining for the loading mechanism.

Designing the feeder prematurely would have risked a footprint that exceeded competition building rules or failed to accommodate the projectile's final 250mm length.

Red Dotted Line: Pre-defined Design Envelope

Comparative Methodology

Option A: Robotic Arm

"High precision and versatility, but resulted in prohibitively slow loading cycles (5.5s+) and complex inverse kinematics."

Option B: Linear Slider

"Inefficient spatial footprint. Stacking three 250mm projectiles linearly would require a 750mm rail, exceeding volume limits."

Selected

Option C: The Revolver

Compact & Symmetrical. Utilizes a circular footprint to house full-size projectiles within the existing rear envelope.

Engineering Insight

Standard methodologies shared a fatal flaw: Full-Tension Feeding. Loading at the maximum stroke unnecessarily stresses the motor and degrades elastic longevity.

The Revolver Concept

Radial Efficiency

Our solution utilizes a rotating carousel that indexes projectiles into a "Ready Position." This circular geometry allows for three full-sized projectiles to be housed within the compact rear quadrant of the launcher.

  • Zero Interference during Pitch/Yaw
  • 360° Symmetrical Indexing
  • Rapid Gravity-Assisted Drop

Autonomous Firing Sequence

A High-Fidelity Coordination of Torque and Timing

01

Index: Feeder revolves to align the next projectile.

02

Soft-Pull: Sled pulls back 25cm to optical endstop.

03

Load: Servo drops projectile onto the carrier sled.

04

Engage: Sled moves forward to hook the projectile nose.

05

Fire: Full pull to 10Nm torque and trigger release.
