Projectile Design

The D.A.R.T. projectile was governed by a brutal constraint envelope: < 350g total mass within a 250 x 150 x 150mm volume. It is a high-G structural vessel designed to shield sensitive embedded flight electronics while providing the aerodynamic authority for mid-air correction.

Iterative Optimization

The Path to Darkstar

Achieving ballistic stability at 25m/s is a task of systematic refinement. Before the V7 Darkstar reached its definitive form, its predecessors underwent an exhaustive computational gauntlet. Through six distinct generations, we mapped the fluid dynamics of every fin geometry, fuselage profile and main body structure.

By leveraging high-fidelity Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), we identified and eliminated critical aerodynamic pitfalls—from early-stage structural flutter in the V1 Thorn to boundary layer separation issues in the V6 Trident. This data-driven evolution was essential to ensure the direct-drive servos could maintain control authority during the high-G terminal phase.

The Endurance Gauntlet

Real-world endurance testing mirrored the computational predictions. After 57 consecutive shots, the TPU 95A prototype exhibited critical tearing at the head-to-fuselage interface and structural fatigue.

Darkstar Performance: Survived 227 high-velocity launches with zero structural failure. Minor superficial scratching was observed on the Carbon Fiber wings, but core control authority remained 100% nominal.

Sensitivity Analysis

As predicted, precision scales linearly up to 12°. Beyond this threshold, we observed Aerodynamic Saturation; the increased induced drag and flow separation on the leeward side of the rudder reduce the efficiency gain per degree. This turbulence accounts for the increased scatter in the high-angle clusters.

Standardized Mass Control

To ensure ballistic consistency across all 227+ test fires, every projectile undergoes a strict mass calibration protocol. By utilizing a modular component system, we normalize each unit to exactly **260g**, ensuring that the software's kinematic turn derivations remain valid regardless of which specific dart is loaded into the launcher.

CAD Animation: Exploded Assembly View

Ergonomic Rigidity

The assembly features an interlocking architecture designed for both field-serviceability and high-impact structural rigidity. The Carbon Fiber wing mainframe snaps into the TPU64D chassis via precision-tolerance mechanical locks. This ergonomic approach allows for rapid tool-less assembly while maintaining the integrity required to survive **16,250 N** of peak impulse force.
