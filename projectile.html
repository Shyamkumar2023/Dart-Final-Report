Projectile Design

The D.A.R.T. projectile is engineered to bridge the gap between high-G mechanical survival and digital flight precision. By fusing the structural rigidity of carbon fiber with a high-impact PEBA90A zonal core, the system is built to withstand the violence of launch while maintaining the aerodynamic agility required for mid-air trajectory correction at a range of 26 meters.
