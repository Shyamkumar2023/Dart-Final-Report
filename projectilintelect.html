Projectile Internal Electronics

The projectile internal electronics were designed around strict space, power, and functionality constraints. The system had to fit fully within the projectile while supporting onboard sensing, image processing, fin actuation, and reliable power distribution.

Design requirements

Battery capacity limit

The projectile battery must not exceed 4 Wh, as this is a competition requirement.

Continuous current supply

The power system must support at least 8 A continuous current so all onboard components can operate under peak load with margin.

Dual voltage rails

The electronics must provide both 5 V and 7.4 V rails, since different components require different supply voltages for proper operation.

Plug-and-play wiring

All connectors and ports must be labelled and fit within the projectile, so damaged parts can be replaced quickly and maintenance stays simple.

Compact PCB form factor

The custom PCB must fit fully inside the projectile. Internal space is limited, so the board has to remain compact.

PCB mounting stability

The PCB should include at least two mounting holes to reduce vibration and lower the risk of electrical failure during operation.

Onboard image processing

The projectile must support onboard colour detection and tracking so it can detect and track the target during operation.

Servo control capability

The control electronics must support reliable servo actuation to drive the projectile fins smoothly and consistently.
